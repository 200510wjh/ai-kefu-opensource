## Style Prompt

Premium founder documentary for Douyin: black glass interface layers, cyan-violet neon, midnight startup office, cinematic contrast, real entrepreneurial atmosphere, sharp SaaS product surfaces, subtle city reflections, no robots, no cartoon characters.

## Colors

- Deep black background: `#05060a`
- Glass panel: `#111827`
- Cold white text: `#f7fbff`
- Cyan neon accent: `#18e7ff`
- Violet neon accent: `#8b5cf6`
- Muted blue-gray copy: `#aeb9c9`

## Typography

- Primary: `Noto Sans SC`
- Technical UI: `JetBrains Mono`

## Motion

- Slow documentary camera drift, parallax, focus pulls, glass panels sliding in.
- Tech transitions use chromatic blur, vertical push, and data-line wipes.
- Captions are clean, bold, and centered in the lower third for Douyin readability.

## What NOT to Do

- No cartoon robots or mascot imagery.
- No colorful toy gradients or cute SaaS illustrations.
- No cluttered icon walls.
- No low-contrast gray captions.
