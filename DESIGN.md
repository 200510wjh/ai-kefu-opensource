# HyperFrames Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

VibeCoding portfolio showreel for an AI commerce SaaS founder product. The experience should feel like a cinematic personal project reveal: black glass canvas, neon magenta/cyan/gold highlights, sharp product UI panels, oversized Chinese headlines, visible workflow cards, and smooth showreel-style motion. It should present the product as a polished AI operating system for merchants, not a plain admin dashboard.

## Colors

- Background: `#050816`, deep black-blue studio canvas
- Surface: `#0F172A`, glass dashboard panels
- Text: `#F8FAFC`, high-contrast white
- Muted: `#94A3B8`, secondary labels
- Neon Pink: `#FF3D9A`, creator energy and active accents
- Neon Cyan: `#22D3EE`, AI/automation signal
- Gold CTA: `#FACC15`, conversion and primary action

## Typography

- Headline: `Microsoft YaHei UI`, 850-900, huge and confident
- UI: `Inter`, `Microsoft YaHei`, system sans-serif, 650-800
- Avoid tiny text in hero frames; every scene must be readable as a screen-recorded portfolio video.

## Motion Rules

- Use scene-based reveals: title, subtitle, cards, then CTA.
- Use GSAP-style staggered entrances, not random bounces.
- Keep motion smooth, cinematic, and product-focused.
- Use light sweeps and timeline pulses to imply HyperFrames/video generation.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not look like a generic SaaS admin template.
- Do not use beige, flat gray, or default blue-only palettes.
- Do not make the first screen a form.
- Do not hide the actual product modules behind marketing copy.
- Do not auto-send messages or imply unsafe automation.
